# Growcend CRM — v4: Job Demands (HR)

## HR / Director
1. Menu → **Job Demands** → **New Demand**.
2. Bharo: Country, Job, Vacancy, Employer, Salary, khana-rehna, experience, age, closing date…
   Rate (employer se mila / agreed) aur Commission (fixed ₹ ya % ) — ye sirf HR/Director ko dikhta hai.
3. **Create Demand** → apne-aap **Assign** tab khulta hai → jin Counsellors / Bittu ko demand deni hai unko tick karo,
   har ek ka alag Rate, Target, Commission likho → **Save Assignment**.
   (Jise tick nahi kiya, use ye demand dikhegi hi nahi.)
4. **Overview** tab → Poster / Demand image / JD PDF upload. Poster ke neeche submit hue candidates ke naam dikhte hain.
5. **Candidates** tab → har candidate ka Interview, Selection, "CV Emailed?" (Yes/No + kisko + kya bheja) update karo.
   Selected karte hi candidate ka status apne-aap "Selected" ho jata hai aur employer details bhar jati hain.
6. Upar ke dabbe apne-aap ginte hain: Submitted, Passports, Interviews, Selected, Rejected, Pending, CV Emailed, Remaining.
7. Demand band karni ho → Edit → Status "Closed" (phir naya candidate submit nahi hoga).

## Counsellor / Bittu
1. Menu → **Job Demands** → sirf aapko di gayi demands.
2. Upar peele dabbe mein: aapka rate, target, commission.
3. **Candidates** tab → apna candidate search karo → **Submit**. Ek candidate ek demand par sirf ek baar.
4. Remarks likh sakte ho, Pending candidate ko withdraw kar sakte ho. Selection sirf HR karega.
5. Bittu: agent ka candidate ho to "B2B Agent name" bhi likho.

## Privacy
- Counsellor ko employer ka email/phone, company ka rate, doosron ke rate aur doosron ke candidates nahi dikhte.
- HR submit hue candidates ki details dekh sakta hai (badal nahi sakta).
- Demand / submission delete sirf Director.
